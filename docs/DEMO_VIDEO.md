# Demo video script (2:45)

Rules from the hackathon: under 3 minutes, public on YouTube, no copyrighted music, show the real app working.
Record the live Render URL with real services on (the sidebar should say paypal-sandbox, claude, channel3, supabase).

**Before recording**

- Click **Reset demo**. The screens start clean, with one grinder already waiting for you. Leave it: it's the
  approve-on-your-phone shot.
- Open your sandbox Personal account at sandbox.paypal.com in another tab, to show the reserved money.
- Have your phone ready, with the camera app open.
- Run the three Claude prompts once beforehand (then Reset again), to check how real Channel3 products come out.
- Cut out every wait for Claude when editing. Prices come from real stores, so say "the beans", not "$14".

Three beats use Claude (coffee, Sam, the control room question); the rest need none.
