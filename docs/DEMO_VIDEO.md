# Demo video script (2:55)

Rules from the hackathon: under 3 minutes, public on YouTube, no copyrighted music, show the real app working.
Record the live Render URL with real services on (**Settings** should say paypal-sandbox, claude, channel3, supabase).

**Before recording**

- Open **Settings** (bottom left) and click **Reset demo**. Ask starts clean, with one grinder already waiting for
  you. Leave it: it's the deny-and-teach shot.
- Open your sandbox Personal account at sandbox.paypal.com in another tab, to show the reserved money.
- Have a terminal ready with the Leash API call from the README, with `"agentName":"Claude Desktop"` and the live URL.
- Run the Claude prompts once beforehand (then Reset again), to check how real Channel3 products come out.
- Cut out every wait for Claude when editing. Prices come from real stores, so say "the beans", not "$14".

Say the spoken beats while holding the space bar, close to the mic, and leave the sound on: Leash answers out loud.
